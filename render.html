Kyiv & Poland · War

Kyiv hit by huge barrage; missiles fall in Poland

More than 400 drones and 110 missiles strike Ukraine, killing at least three people in Kyiv and knocking out power across ten regions. Two missiles and a suspected drone land in eastern Poland; NATO calls the incursion grave.

Markets · World

Stocks slide into correction as AI shares tumble

The S&P 500 drops 3.7%, entering correction territory as AI stocks fall, while the Nasdaq slips negative for the year. Japan’s Nikkei sinks 5.1% and South Korea’s Kospi more than 5%.

UN climate negotiations run past their Friday deadline still without a deal, divided over fossil fuels and adaptation funding for poorer countries.

Washington · USA

FBI investigates breach of its crime database

The FBI is looking into an apparent intrusion into its National Crime Information Center database; a US farm-payments system was reportedly breached as well.

Copenhagen · Denmark

Denmark says Russia plotted rapper’s murder

Denmark detains two men and orders a Russian defence attaché out of the country over an alleged plot to kill a US-Ukrainian rapper.

Space · Mercury

BepiColombo finally settles into Mercury orbit

Eight years and 10 billion km after launch, the European–Japanese probe fires its engines and enters orbit around Mercury — only the second spacecraft ever to circle it.
